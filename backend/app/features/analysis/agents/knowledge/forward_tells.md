# Forward tells (WhatsApp-viral misinformation, India-first)

Distilled from Indian fact-checker practice (Alt News, BOOM, PIB unit)
and WhatsApp-verification guides. Context cues, not pixel proof.

## Forward-shape red flags

- "Forwarded many times" label + urgent framing ("share before deleted").
- Vague attribution ("a friend in the department sent this"), no outlet link.
- Emotional trigger first, claim second (outrage, pride, fear, communal bait).

## Recycled-content patterns

- Old images recaptioned to fresh events (floods, blasts, elections, schemes).
- Clipped videos missing start/end; location cues contradict claimed place.
- Screenshots of "news" with no masthead, date, or URL — pure text cards.

## India-specific claim shapes

- Fake government schemes (free scooters, cash transfers) in officialese.
- Doctored quotes on politician images; party symbols pasted at clip ends.
- Deepfaked celebrity/politician endorsements around elections and crises.
- Cross-border propaganda clips impersonating officials (PIB-busted pattern).

## Verification moves (mirror in search queries)

- Reverse-search the image; check earliest appearance date vs claimed date.
- Search the core claim + "fact check" + site (altnews.in, boomlive.in).
- Major claim absent from mainstream outlets and fact-checkers: likely fake.
- E2E encryption means no platform label will save the user — say so plainly.
