# AI-image tells (diffusion-era, 2024-2026)

Distilled from photorealism/artifact research and practitioner detectors.
Heuristic cues only — always corroborate with instrument scores and context.

## Hands and limbs

- Extra, missing, or fused fingers; finger count changes between hands.
- Hands melting into objects (cups, railings); watches/bracelets fused to skin.
- Arms bending at impossible joints; legs duplicated behind crowds.

## Text and logos

- Rendered text garbled, mirrored, or pseudo-script beyond ~6 characters.
- Logos close-but-wrong (letter swaps, extra strokes); watermarks as smudges.
- Signboards legible at a glance but nonsense on close reading.

## Faces (AI portraits)

- Overly smooth "beauty-filter" skin next to hyper-detailed hair/strands.
- Asymmetric earrings, glasses arms vanishing into temples, teeth merging.
- Pupils non-round or mismatched catchlights between eyes.

## Light and geometry

- Shadows falling in contradictory directions; reflections showing wrong scenes.
- Tiled/repeated textures (bricks, leaves, crowd faces) with visible rhythm.
- Background strangers with smeared or half-rendered faces near image edges.

## Compression signature (pairs with ELA tool)

- Fully synthetic images often show UNIFORM error response (no camera PRNU).
- Splices of AI content into real photos light up as mismatched ELA regions.
- Screenshots/re-encodes of AI images flatten tells: distrust clean metadata.
